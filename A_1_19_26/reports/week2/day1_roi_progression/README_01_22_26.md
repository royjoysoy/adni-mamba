# Week 2 – Day 1: ROI-based Progression Ground Truth (01-22-2026)

## Goal
Establish ROI-based progression metrics independent of model training,
to validate whether CNN / Mamba models capture **subject-specific disease progression speed**.

These ROI-derived metrics are treated as *ground-truth proxies* and are **not used as model inputs**.

---

## Key Design Decisions
- ROI features are **NOT** used as inputs to CNN or Mamba.
- ICV is used for normalization (ICV missing = 0).
- **No imputation** is performed for follow-up ROI values.
- Baseline ROI values are used as within-subject references.
- Missing ROI values lead to NaN slopes and are excluded *only from ROI analyses*.

---

## ROIs Analyzed
**Primary ROIs**
- Ventricles
- WholeBrain

**Secondary ROIs (subset analysis)**
- Hippocampus
- Entorhinal

---

## Method: Longitudinal ROI Slope
For each subject and ROI:

1. **ICV normalization**
ROI_norm = ROI / ICV


2. **Baseline-referenced change**
ΔROI = ROI_norm - ROI_norm_bl


3. **Progression slope**
slope = ΔROI / Month_bl


This slope represents *per-month structural change* and serves as a proxy for
individual disease progression speed.

---

## Robustness Check: Fixed 6-Month Window
To avoid slope inflation from very short follow-up intervals:

- A fixed-window analysis was performed using visits closest to **6 months (± tolerance)**.
- Slope was recomputed as:
slope_6m = (ROI_6m_norm - ROI_bl_norm) / 6
- Scatter plots were generated for:
- All subjects
- CN
- MCI
- AD

This serves as a robustness check against denominator-driven artifacts.

## Interpretation Notes
- Large slopes at very short Month_bl are expected due to small denominators.
- Fixed-window analysis confirms that diagnostic group differences persist
  beyond early follow-up artifacts.
- ROI slopes are treated as *evaluation targets*, not predictive features.
